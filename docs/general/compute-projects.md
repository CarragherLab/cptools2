# Compute Projects

> **Source:** <https://www.wiki.ed.ac.uk/display/ResearchServices/Compute+Projects>

## Overview

Where grant funding is not available, users can run **low priority jobs on Eddie for free**. However, all users are encouraged to fund the cost of paid compute projects from research grants wherever possible.

Two types of compute project are available: **Ringfences** and **Priority Compute** projects. Both are charged by CPU hours (see [Charges](https://www.wiki.ed.ac.uk/display/ResearchServices/Charges)). GPUs and RAM are not charged at the moment.

## Ringfence

Ringfences are better for users who need **exclusive and immediate access** to nodes for a specified amount of time (e.g. to meet a short-term deadline), or for users with very particular requirements not met by the standard scheduler configuration (e.g. very long runtimes over 48 hours or specific virtual memory requirements).

**Benefits:** Jobs effectively don't queue; purchased resources are exclusively available to you and your group.

**Drawbacks:** You pay for the ringfenced resource for the entire specified period whether you use it or not — generally much more expensive. Minimum ringfence is entire nodes (32 cores minimum).

## Priority Compute

Priority compute is better for users who want **priority access to the Eddie queue** as a long-term arrangement, and whose jobs fit into the standard node configuration.

**Benefits:** You purchase priority compute upfront but don't have to use it within any specified period. Your balance simply reduces as you use compute time. When the balance reaches 0, your priority reduces to the free baseline. You can top up via the Storage Manager.

## Requesting a Compute Project

Use the request form linked from the wiki. Complete the fields as follows:

- **Budget Holder:** The person who owns the funds (usually the group PI). Recommended that requests are submitted by the budget holder.
- **Billing Contact:** The person to contact about billing (can be the same as the budget holder, or a departmental finance team member).
- **Project Name:** Any name you choose. Priority projects will be prefixed with your School name; ringfences with 'ringfence'.
- **AD Group:** The Active Directory group controlling access to your project (typically associated with your group storage on DataStore and Eddie).
- **Core Hours:** Default is 10,000 hours. For ringfences, calculate as: total period hours × number of cores (minimum 32).
- **Start/End Date:** Only relevant for ringfences with specified dates. Leave defaults for priority projects.

For further advice, contact us via the IS Helpline.
