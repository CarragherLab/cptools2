# How to Cite Eddie in Publications

> **Source:** <https://www.wiki.ed.ac.uk/display/ResearchServices/How+to+cite+Eddie+in+publications>

## Acknowledgement

> This work has made use of the resources provided by the Edinburgh Compute and Data Facility (ECDF) (<http://www.ecdf.ed.ac.uk/>).

## Citation

To cite the ECDF website (replace the date below with the date on which you referred to the website):

> Edinburgh Compute and Data Facility web site. 1 December 2021. U of Edinburgh <www.ecdf.ed.ac.uk>.

Or the shortened version:

> ECDF. 1 December 2021. U of Edinburgh <www.ecdf.ed.ac.uk>.
